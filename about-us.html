Our Story

From creative studio
to performance agency.

Cyber Design was founded in 2011 by Syed Murtaza Razzak in Karachi with a simple belief: that great design and smart marketing should work together — not separately. In the early years we focused on branding, web design and content for local businesses.

As Pakistan’s e-commerce landscape grew, we evolved. We built deep expertise in Meta advertising and Shopify development, and began managing flagship brands at scale. The results spoke for themselves — 200,000+ purchases, PKR 930M+ in tracked sales, ROAS consistently between 5× and 11×.

Today Cyber Design operates as a full-stack growth agency: we design the store, produce the creative, build the brand, and scale it on Meta. One team. One funnel. Accountable end to end.

Our Approach

We believe the store and the ads must work as one system. We design the conversion funnel end to end — which is why our ROAS numbers hold up at scale.
